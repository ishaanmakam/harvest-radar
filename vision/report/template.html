Decision

Field outlines come from the pipeline's segmentation, and farm plans use source: vegetation_index. Perceptron's job is to describe and date what changed in each zone, in plain words. That output is in data/out/insights.json.

Test 1 · ships

Zone summaries

For each of the 9 zones we cut the same ~3 km square out of the 6 clearest passes (cloud-free share at least 85%: Aug 28, Sep 2, Oct 2, Oct 5, Oct 10, Oct 17), enlarged each to 768 px, and asked Perceptron which parts changed and when. Below, three of those six passes per zone, Perceptron's own words, its flood / stress / change answers, and our check by eye against the frames.

Z01 · north-west

Mostly right

Check: centre and south harvests are real. It misses the large harvested fields along the north edge.

What this tells us

The narration is grounded: dates and directions mostly match, and it never invented flooding or crop damage. Its usual mistake is leaving out one harvested area, and twice it described the wrong part of the zone. Good enough to show as a plain-language note beside each zone. The per-zone counts it also returns (for example "standing=10 harvested=25") are rough guesses and are not shown as facts.

Test 2 · not usable

Field outlines

The spec's first-hour gate: can Perceptron draw field boundaries well enough to replace segmentation? Left, Perceptron's polygons on the Oct 17 frame. Right, the pipeline's segmentation outlines on the same frame, for comparison (orange corn, green soybean).

71full-frame polygons
8,192output tokens used (hit the limit)
401tile polygons
206tile duplicates
99.5%tile shapes starting on the left or top edge
Why it fails

Perceptron lists shapes the way it reads text: left to right, top to bottom, until it runs out of output room. The shapes are evenly spaced guesses, not traced edges. Enlarging the image did not help. This is the same pattern we saw on the synthetic test frame earlier in the night.

Test 3 · not usable

Asked to box every field as "standing" or "harvested" on the Oct 17 frame, then count each.

Answer

standing=196 harvested=0

Wrong. The Oct 17 frame has many light-tan harvested fields, visible in the north, centre-west and south-west. The box layout is the same row-by-row scan as the outlines.

Test 4 · accurate

Change across dates

Four whole-frame passes in date order, with one plain question: how do the fields change, and where?

“There is no evidence of field burning. The main change is progressive drying and harvest… Notable harvested/tan areas include a small patch in the upper-central part of the scene by image 2, a larger tan area in the lower-left quadrant by image 3, and additional tan/bare patches in the central-west and central-east areas by image 3.”Perceptron, multi-date change probe (shortened)

Check

Correct on every point we can verify: no burn scars, a general dry-down, and new harvested ground in the lower-left, centre-west and centre-east by Oct 17. This is the result that made narration Perceptron's job.

Test 5 · off by default

Per-farm change boxes

With Niranjan's real field outlines in hand, we gave the per-farm alerts one more try on zone Z01: box what changed between Oct 10 and Oct 17, then match each box to a farm. Our safeguards first asked a yes/no question (flood no, stress no, change yes), so only change boxes were requested. In zone Z07 the same run produced a scan pattern, which the safeguards threw out.

Verdict

5 clear new harvests, 2 partly right, 3 on fields that had not changed. Far better than on the synthetic frame, where every box was junk, but a 3-in-10 false alarm rate is too high for a message to a farmer. The index math already catches a harvest as a sharp drop in greenness, so these boxes add risk without adding coverage. They stay available behind --cues.

Earlier · synthetic frame

What the first tests taught us

Before real imagery arrived we tested on the team's synthetic stand-in frame. The same failure patterns showed up there first, which is why the safeguards existed before the real test.

  • Outlines: 112 polygons, all in a thin strip along the top edge, cut off at the output limit.
  • State boxes: 145 tiny boxes in the same strip, all labelled "burned", with no counts.
  • Per-farm boxes, first version: the same box repeated as "flood" while the narration said "no clear flooding", and a column scanned down the left edge as "change". 14 alerts, all false.
  • Fixes: ask a yes/no question first and request boxes only for a "yes", throw out duplicate and edge-scan patterns, enlarge crops. On the synthetic frame this cut false alerts to zero.
  • Narration: sensible from the first run, which held up on real imagery.

Method

How we checked

  • Every verdict above was made by looking at the images, not from the model's own numbers. Overlay pictures were drawn from the model's raw coordinates.
  • Zone checks compare Perceptron's sentence with the Aug 28, Oct 2 and Oct 17 crops shown beside it.
  • Per-farm boxes were judged one by one on Oct 10 vs Oct 17 close-ups.
  • Prompts describe the real landscape (Iowa corn and soybeans, where October browning is normal) from the team's region config.
  • Raw responses are kept in insights.json (raw) and data/out/perceptron/validation/results.json.
